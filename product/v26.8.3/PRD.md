# Product Requirements Document — ggen-legacy v26.8.3

## Claims Reconciliation

This document defines the v26.8.3 candidate target for the **Executable Enterprise Architecture Foundry Corpus** under the current repository constitution. The current exact baseline is `70e599a599fedb7c62c965377cc2f80df1fa01ec`. The PRD/ARD bundle may reach `ALIVE` through independent ggen verification; that does not promote the complete A–K foundry, ecosystem, production, certification, release or sunset crown.

## Product Thesis

ggen-legacy converts repository and ecosystem history into executable architecture authority, reusable solution assets, equivalence evidence, independent verification and standing decisions.

## Repository Role

ggen-legacy owns corpus, lineage, capability realizations, primitives, packs, equivalence portfolios, reference receivers and independent verification. ggen remains the manufacturing kernel. ggen-legacy has no direct actuation authority.

## Customer Outcomes

Customers receive archaeology graphs, capability ledgers, lineage, dispositions, reusable packs, reference implementations, equivalence evidence, verifier appliances, offline bundles, clean-room replay, Release Admission and separately computed Sunset Admission.

### GL-FR-002 — Multi-head preservation

### GL-FR-004 — Complete provenance

Owner: **Lineage Store**. Acceptance: Every extraction binds source, destination, digest, rationale and recovery.

### GL-FR-005 — Disposition closure

Owner: **Disposition Engine**. Acceptance: Every final capability has exactly one lawful disposition.

### GL-FR-006 — Architecture primitive extraction

Owner: **Primitive Foundry**. Acceptance: Generalization preserves origin and unique constraints.

### GL-FR-007 — Solution pack manufacture

Owner: **Pack Foundry**. Acceptance: Packs bind capabilities, dependencies, controls, verifiers and receipts.

### GL-FR-008 — Repository reconstitution plan

Owner: **Planning Controller**. Acceptance: Each repository has a deterministic dependency-closed plan.

### GL-FR-009 — Exact-source witness

### GL-FR-010 — Repository-owned validation

Owner: **Validation Adapter**. Acceptance: Repository-native commands retain authority.

Owner: **Constitution**. Acceptance: Zero findings and unexecuted observation remain distinct.

## Nonfunctional Requirements

The corpus must be deterministic, source-isolated, least-privilege, portable, checkpointable, clean-room replayable and customer-exportable. It must preserve multi-head history and refuse absolute-path or hidden-sibling assumptions in release-critical rails.

## Exclusions

v26.8.3 does not claim recovered human intent, universal equivalence, direct production actuation, external production standing, physical-device standing through emulation, certification or predecessor retirement without subject-specific Sunset Admission.

## Launch Theorem

The PRD/ARD authority bundle launches when exact source identity, document digests, full requirement traceability, component ownership, verifier assignment, zero direct actuation, zero self-certification, zero replay differences and independent ggen verification close. Product and repository crowns remain separate.
