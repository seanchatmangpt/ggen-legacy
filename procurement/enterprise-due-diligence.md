# Enterprise Due-Diligence Package

## Product and company

Provide category, scope, ownership, roadmap, release policy, support model, financial diligence, insurance, business continuity, and termination/export procedures.

## Security and privacy

Provide architecture and data flows, trust boundaries, threat model, vulnerability-management evidence, penetration-test results when available, subprocessor inventory, residency, encryption/key policy, incident response, retention, deletion, legal hold, and customer export.

## Engineering assurance

Provide SDLC, change control, exact-head CI, verification ladder, negative fixtures, SBOM, dependency and license policy, build provenance, artifact quarantine, receipts, replay, SLO definitions, resilience tests, and DR receipts.

## Current Project 001 disclosure

Available: product and architecture authority, governance, mdBook, threat model, operations targets, procurement checklist, JSON authority, schemas, fixtures, local documentation verifier, and exact-head workflow definition.

Not yet evidenced: production implementation, external audit, penetration test, production benchmarks, uptime history, customer references, production SLO attainment, or external Sunset Admission.

## License and IP

The repository’s observed initial commit includes an MIT license. Customer source, credentials, evidence, and generated customer artifacts retain originating rights and contractual restrictions. External packs expose source, digest, version, license, transitive dependencies, consumer capabilities, and substitution rules.

## Compliance boundary

The package may map controls, assemble evidence, and identify exceptions. It does not claim certification or compliance without an independent authority.
