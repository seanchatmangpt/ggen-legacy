# Working Backwards PR/FAQ — ggen-legacy v26.8.1

## Customer promise

**ggen-legacy reconstructs what a legacy repository actually means, manufactures its replacement from machine-readable authority, and proves whether the original implementation can be retired.**

## Why now?

AI coding systems increase the rate at which replacement code can be produced. They do not automatically establish historical contract completeness, refusal behavior, provenance, equivalence, replay, or safe retirement. The faster code is generated, the more valuable an independent evidence and standing layer becomes.

## Who buys it?

CIOs, CTOs, architecture and platform executives, modernization leaders, and business owners carrying operational or regulatory risk.

## What is the core insight?

The customer does not ultimately want a rewrite. The customer wants to stop carrying the cost, ambiguity, and operational risk of the old system. A rewrite is an intermediate artifact. A defensible retirement decision is the outcome.

## What is the moat?

The integrated object graph connecting historical capability, provenance, observable contract, authority, projection, implementation ownership, equivalence case, positive witness, negative falsifier, receipt, replay, standing, and Sunset Admission.

## What is the smallest viable release?

One bounded repository moving through exact-source resolution, exhaustive declared observation, capability admission, deterministic manufacture, complete disposition, positive and negative equivalence, independent verification, clean-room replay, Release Admission, and Sunset Admission or typed refusal.

## What is available in Project 001?

The Fortune 5-grade product and architecture corpus, machine-readable bootstrap authority, schemas, fixtures, documentation verifier, exact-head workflow, source-admission backlog, and explicit claim register.

## What is not claimed?

Universal repository understanding, production implementation `ALIVE`, unrestricted semantic equivalence, guaranteed retirement, Fortune 5 customer deployment, security certification, or regulatory compliance.

## How is completion measured?

Not by files, code volume, agent completion, deployment, or elapsed time. Completion is computed from zero unknown final capabilities, zero unknown dispositions, zero missing verifiers or equivalence cases, zero replay differences, admitted release, admitted sunset, and independent `ALIVE` standing for the declared scope.
