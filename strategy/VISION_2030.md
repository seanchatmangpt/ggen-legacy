# Vision 2030 — From Repositories to Executable Manufacturing Authorities

By 2030, a critical software repository is no longer treated as a collection of files maintained through accumulated human memory. It is an executable projection of admitted authority.

Requirements, capabilities, interfaces, behaviors, refusals, evidence, migration state, and release standing are machine-readable. Source, tests, configuration, diagrams, plans, reports, and receipts are manufactured from that authority and independently verified against it.

Legacy modernization no longer ends when replacement code reaches production. It ends when behavioral closure is proven and the former implementation receives a machine-readable Sunset Admission.

## 2030 outcomes

1. Repository meaning is explicit and provenance-bound.
2. One semantic fact drives all lawful dependent projections.
3. Modernization claims are falsifiable through witnesses, negative fixtures, receipt validation, and replay.
4. Legacy retirement is an engineering admission decision rather than a schedule event.
5. Agents become governed manufacturers, not unbounded actuators or self-certifiers.
6. Verification is part of repository architecture rather than a disconnected final phase.
7. Ontologies, observers, projectors, adapters, packs, and verifier families become reusable enterprise capital.

## Horizon

- **2026:** prove Project 001 and admit the first implementation phase.
- **2027:** productize observation, authority, and verifier families.
- **2028:** scale Repository Foundry Operations across estates.
- **2029:** establish independent certification procedures and external reconstitution cases.
- **2030:** make Verified Repository Reconstitution the expected completion discipline for critical legacy retirement.

> Give us the software history, and we will reconstruct its contract. Give us the admitted contract, and we will manufacture the repository. Give us the manufactured repository, and we will test its claims. Give us the evidence, and we will compute its standing. When the proof closes, we will show whether the legacy system may be retired.
