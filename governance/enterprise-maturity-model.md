# Enterprise Maturity Model — 21 Dimensions / 63 Proof Obligations

Every dimension has exactly three conjunctive obligations:

- **P — Positive:** demonstrate declared behavior at an exact coordinate.
- **N — Negative:** trigger a same-object violation and observe typed refusal or bounded failure.
- **R — Replay:** re-execute from the same admitted coordinate and verify equivalent evidence and standing.

## EM-01 Strategy alignment

Business outcome, modernization thesis, scope, and nonclaims are explicit. Obligations: `EM-01-P/N/R`.

## EM-02 Operating model

Diversification, Coordination, Replication, or Unification assumptions are declared. Obligations: `EM-02-P/N/R`.

## EM-03 Capability ownership

Every capability has one accountable owner. Obligations: `EM-03-P/N/R`.

## EM-04 Semantic authority

Every fact and exclusive output has one semantic owner or named merge law. Obligations: `EM-04-P/N/R`.

## EM-05 Data governance

Classification, provenance, residency, retention, deletion, and legal hold are explicit. Obligations: `EM-05-P/N/R`.

## EM-06 Application architecture

Repository, module, service, and consumer boundaries are declared. Obligations: `EM-06-P/N/R`.

## EM-07 Technology architecture

Runtime, storage, network, deployment, and toolchain coordinates are bounded. Obligations: `EM-07-P/N/R`.

## EM-08 Integration architecture

Protocols, schemas, identity, retries, idempotency, and failure semantics are explicit. Obligations: `EM-08-P/N/R`.

## EM-09 Identity and access

Human and workload identity use least privilege and separation of duties. Obligations: `EM-09-P/N/R`.

## EM-10 Security engineering

Threats, controls, abuse cases, and negative fixtures are maintained. Obligations: `EM-10-P/N/R`.

## EM-11 Privacy

Purpose limitation, minimization, subject rights, and deletion boundaries are explicit. Obligations: `EM-11-P/N/R`.

## EM-12 Compliance evidence

Controls map to evidence and exceptions without claiming certification. Obligations: `EM-12-P/N/R`.

## EM-13 Resilience

Fault domains, retries, checkpoints, degradation, and chaos cases are tested. Obligations: `EM-13-P/N/R`.

## EM-14 Recovery

RTO, RPO, backup integrity, restore, and replay are proven. Obligations: `EM-14-P/N/R`.

## EM-15 Observability

Events, metrics, traces, logs, state, and causal linkage cover the same operation. Obligations: `EM-15-P/N/R`.

## EM-16 Performance and scale

Workload, environment, limits, variance, and benchmark evidence are published. Obligations: `EM-16-P/N/R`.

## EM-17 Software supply chain

Dependencies, SBOM, provenance, signatures, vulnerabilities, licenses, and quarantine are governed. Obligations: `EM-17-P/N/R`.

## EM-18 Change and release

Exact-head verification, promotion authority, rollback, and evidence retention are enforced. Obligations: `EM-18-P/N/R`.

## EM-19 Portfolio economics

Cost, capacity, Little’s Law, WIP, evidence debt, and retirement economics are visible. Obligations: `EM-19-P/N/R`.

## EM-20 Workforce and governance

RACI, skills, review, exceptions, support, and escalation are explicit. Obligations: `EM-20-P/N/R`.

## EM-21 Evidence-bearing self-governance

The system can observe, diagnose, plan, emit intent, verify, receipt, and replay without granting itself DO or standing. Obligations: `EM-21-P/N/R`.

All 63 obligations are conjunctive for the declared enterprise crown. Unattempted obligations remain `UNKNOWN`. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires explicit authority and a verifier; absence is insufficient.
