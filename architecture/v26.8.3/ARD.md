# Architecture Requirements Document — ggen-legacy v26.8.3

## Authority

This ARD operationalizes the v26.8.3 candidate PRD without widening the current repository constitution. Authored authority, controller-owned generated state, evidence and explanatory projections remain separate.

## Architecture Objective

The target is an immutable provenance-bound corpus plus executable reconstitution and independent-assurance foundry: history → observation → admission → disposition → primitive extraction → pack manufacture → equivalence → verification → replay → release/sunset decisions.

## Repository Topology

ggen is the manufacturing kernel. ggen-legacy is the corpus and independent receiver. Product repositories remain independent operational authorities. Transport lineage never gains product authority.

## Interfaces

The kernel/corpus protocol binds exact repositories, revisions, trees, authority and corpus digests, toolchain, pack identity, receipt schema, ticket and claim ceiling. Source import and manufacture/receiver directions are separately receipted.

## Trust Boundaries

Untrusted source, observation sandbox, semantic admission, authored corpus, generated foundry state, reference manufacture, immutable evidence, independent verifier, customer environment, production/device and sunset authorization are distinct boundaries.

## Verification Architecture

The independent verifier rederives exact identity, schemas, requirement traceability, positive and negative evidence, receipt integrity and replay. Product receiver evidence may not cross-promote device, production, certification or retirement standing.

## Acceptance Architecture

Every PRD requirement maps to a named non-actuating component, owner, verifier and acceptance. The corpus must refuse direct actuation, transport promotion, self-certification, missing disposition, missing source identity and replay differences. ggen must independently return `ALIVE` for this authority bundle.
