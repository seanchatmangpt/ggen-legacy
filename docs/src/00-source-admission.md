## Statement classes

- **Normative requirement:** what a conforming implementation must do.
- **Admitted design:** accepted v26.8.1 architecture or policy.
- **Observed evidence:** what a verifier actually executed.
- **Target posture:** enterprise capability intended but not yet proven.
- **Nonclaim:** what the source set explicitly refuses to assert.

No chapter may silently promote target posture into observed execution.

Fortune 5-grade means the decision surface is complete: business outcome, product, enterprise architecture, governance, security, privacy, compliance evidence, deployment, integration, observability, performance, resilience, recovery, support, procurement, supply chain, release, and retirement. It does not mean a Fortune 5 company has deployed the product.
